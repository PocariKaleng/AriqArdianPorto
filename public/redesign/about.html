Biography

Hey, I'm Ariq, an Information Technology student at Universitas Brawijaya interested in cryptography and blockchain, which I mostly learn through capture-the-flag competitions. I write up every solve; eleven of those reports, 1,131 pages in total, are on this site.

Since October 2025 that has meant two podiums (SRIFOTON 6.0 and Recursion 2.0), six finals and the best writeup award at TECHCOMFEST 2026.

In March 2026 I started writing challenges myself. As a problem setter I have built rounds for POROS recruitment, the Cyber Hero Battlegrounds qualifiers and both rounds of HOLOGY 9. Since September 2026 I lead Hack It Braw, after a year as a member.

I also build the tools I need along the way: SecureVault, a LaTeX equation tool and a flag generator.

Off the clock: Japanese music on repeat (Yorushika, Kenshi Yonezu, ZUTOMAYO) and anime like Evangelion and Death Note.

Reach me at artalarik2017@gmail.com or on LinkedIn.
